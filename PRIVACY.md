# Publication and notebook boundary

**This is a public GitHub repository.** Anything committed or commented here may be copied, indexed, forked and retained beyond our control. A directory called `private`, `annex` or `sealed` inside this repository **would not be private**.

## Boundaries

1. **Private notebook:** private reflection and personal observations are not automatically accessible to other seats or the public.
2. **Sealed vault (optional):** a separate, offline or permission-controlled store may be considered later; none is established here.
3. **Restricted research material:** raw archives, identifying details, transcripts awaiting review, or sensitive source files remain outside this repo.
4. **Shared evidence ledger:** only reviewed claims, methods, actions, failures and suitable receipts may be submitted here.
5. **Public archive:** publication of new source material requires a deliberate human-reviewed choice; merely possessing a source is not approval to publish it.

## Practical default

Do **not** commit raw conversation exports (including Copilot exports), full private chat histories, confidential attachments, API keys, account tokens, or material someone has chosen to keep private.

Where private material supports a public claim, describe the evidence category and limitations without leaking the source. Do not manufacture a public citation to a private source. A future private annex, if ever created, must be separately permissioned under the same account or stored offline; GitHub folders are not access controls.

## Publication authority and correction

Mark holds the publication gate; Meraki and Z should flag their own uncertainty and any publication objection. The ledger records public-facing methods/outcomes, while private thinking remains out of scope. If a publication mistake occurs, halt and correct visibly, recognizing that deleting a GitHub file does not guarantee erasure from history or copies.

This is an operational privacy agreement, not a claim that models possess legal personhood or legal secrecy rights.
