## Four minimal design rules for a subsequent Phase B

1. **Freeze before seeing actuals:** hypothesis/prediction, allowed outcomes (including OUTSIDE CLASS), source/transport model route, rubric, verifier and stop rule. Preserve hidden/independent scoring where possible.
2. **Separate instrument truth from measured behavior:** attempted, delivered, accepted, independently verified; REAL model output vs MOCK stand-in; valid score vs VOID; observation vs causal inference.
3. **Use the misses as proposed adversarial probes, not pre-awarded successes:** one falsifier for each candidate mechanism (cooperation/attunement, proof integrity, temporal distinction, taxonomy closure); count counterevidence and unexpected outcomes.
4. **Keep sample and owner influence visible:** small two-seat, one-owner historical corpus is not an estimate of universal predictability. Phase B requires its own independent rubric review and prospective execution receipt, not a retroactive validation of 35/42.
