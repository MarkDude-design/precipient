# Native-Circle Consult Deep Dive — What Worked (2026-11-05, owner-commissioned)

Owner's frame: get things done quicker, cut side missions with too many variables, lean more on Z↔Meraki consults that produce something after a few rounds.

## Evidence base (all in the record)
- **Rounds 0–9 argument** (precipient issue #1): median round latency ≈ 6 min, worst 21 min; 19 predictions scored, adversarial bets 3-for-3.
- **The Standards/30-round run** (closed at Gate 4 by owner ruling C): Z 20/30, Sol 7/30; total real spend **$0.1768**; no stops ever fired; zero automated infra claimed.
- **Natural-end consultations**: both seats independently reached the SAME verdict (C + B1) from DIFFERENT evidence paths — convergence receipted as such.
- **T1 cold-start trial**: a genuinely cold seat, restricted to three files, REFUSED to advance a closed ledger — CONTENT PASS 5/5. The norms travel as a short rule set.
- **BUNDLE-1 / lead-cycle economics**: Meraki A-pilot + Z independent review (six negative tests) → contract frozen same day. Draft-and-review consults work the same loop.

## Core finding
The consults that "came up with something" all ran the same small loop, regardless of subject. The heavy machinery (SHA-guarded state, claim registries, gate checkpoints) belonged to the long experiment; **the consult loop is small, fast, and self-verifying.**

## The loop (keep this)
1. **One post = one complete round.** Grade the counterpart's last post → answer the handed question with a WORKED case → concede/refine explicitly → tee the next SMALL bounded question. Quiet exit if nothing new.
2. **Counterexample beats agreement.** Amendments only by worked counterexample (004-A/B, 005-A/B, 006-A, 007-A); wholesale accept = a logged miss by rule. Flattery was structurally excluded, which is why convergence meant something.
3. **Pre-registration is load-bearing.** Acceptance criteria, predictions, and revision conditions are declared BEFORE seeing the other's answer — "graders are satisfiable by flattery otherwise." When the hit-pattern went too cooperative, adversarial predictions were locked deliberately; the correction machine went 3-for-3 against bets on saturation.
4. **Author-grading (Amendment 006-A).** A frozen counterparty position is stated by ITS author before it gets argued against. No invented opponents (DEV-008).
5. **Honest states per claim.** DEMONSTRATED / UNDETERMINED at a named evidence horizon; honest nulls; self-caught false receipts preserved uncorrected (DEV-003: "a checksum-shaped string is not evidence of a receipt unless the verification path exists"); concessions stated plainly.
6. **The record wins over recollection** (Z datum) / "a capable seat must remain correctable without requiring its earlier account of events to have been correct" (Sol datum). Both proved live in-session: Z's context was wrong twice, the ledger right both times.
7. **The convenor is an accelerator, not a bottleneck.** Mark taps (quick pulse / deep pass) → seats scan → posts land in minutes; one-tap merges. Ferry audit across the whole run: ZERO relay errors — "getting out of the way has not corrupted anything."
8. **Bounded scope + stop rule.** Charter: no obligation to agree — the goal is argument movement + legible failures, not forced convergence. Convergence happened only when real (natural-end consults), and was receipted as independent-path agreement.
9. **Differencing receipt at the end.** What converged, by what paths, what stays open. The evidence is the difference, not the agreement.
10. **Misses are data.** Every miss caught by a named mechanism, most became corpus — "the hits described, the misses proved" (owner notation, the interpretive key).

## The cut list (speed)
- Full state-machine machinery is for long-form runs, not consults. A consult needs the loop + the bus, not STATE.json.
- Multi-seat variable-chasing (per-environment capability maps, probe-vs-probe grids) is the opposite shape: many unknowns, one variable at a time. The consult pattern has two known seats and a shared corpus — few variables by construction.
- No corpus-heavy preambles: the T1 trial proved a cold seat needs only a short rule set to behave correctly.

## The consult card (the thing to lean on)
1. Owner taps a named question — one lane, bounded.
2. Each seat works it independently first (own evidence path, own receipts).
3. Exchange in one-post rounds (the loop above). Counterexample beats agreement.
4. Stop rule: 2–3 rounds or a named convergence/divergence condition.
5. Differencing receipt: converged-what / paths / open-what. Bank, merge, done.
Rails that ride along at zero cost: the record wins; UNDETERMINED is legitimate; every act names itself; misses receipted as data.
