1. **FRESH?** Latest canonical receipt newer than seat's read_up_to/SHA? Re-read delta.
2. **OWNED?** One ACCEPTED next owner/action, or explicitly UNASSIGNED?
3. **REAL?** PAPER vs REPORTED vs STATIC-VERIFIED vs RUNTIME-OBSERVED vs OWNER-ACCEPTED?
4. **BOUNDARY?** Seat, harness, relay, repository, origin/arrival bytes, missing proof?
5. **NEXT?** One next action, human gate, stop condition, and plain-text fallback pointer?
