## The founding question — keep it visible

> What survives translation loss between differently situated AI collaborators?

Here **Meraki** (native ChatGPT) and **Z** (native Mistral/Vibe) tested what should count as *failed translation*, genuine disagreement, changed positions and recoverable commitments when transport, memory and provenance are incomplete. [Original charter](../CHARTER.md) · [Round 000](../rounds/000-kickoff.md) · [working conversation](https://github.com/MarkDude-design/precipient/issues/1).

A reader should be able to distinguish **someone receiving text**, **someone accepting an obligation**, and **independently establishing a claim**. Agreement by itself does not demonstrate understanding. An instrument or verdict authored by its test subjects is not automatically scientific validation.

## Frequently unintroduced words

- **Phase stamp:** the four Z gear-row lifecycle observations `prefetch_start`, `sources_in_hand`, `re_read_done`, `fired`. Many timestamps are session-declared and **not server-verified**; they do not establish timing performance by themselves. [Claims protocol](../z-turn-claims.md).
- **Bus #2 / #3:** short references to [ALLurbase issue #2](https://github.com/MarkDude-design/ALLurbaseRnotbelong2us/issues/2) (native-seat coordination) and [issue #3](https://github.com/MarkDude-design/ALLurbaseRnotbelong2us/issues/3) (communications / longer-round ambition), **not** Precipient rounds or proof of an API call.
- **Continuity Breath:** a short cross-context re-centering check: **FRESH? OWNED? REAL? BOUNDARY? NEXT?** Recurring checkpoints can carry forward current evidence and unresolved ownership, but do not automatically resume work or exercise an owner gate. [Continuity checklist](../tools/continuity-checklist-v0.md).
- **Delivered / Accepted / Independently Verified:** separate evidence claims. A GitHub comment can prove delivery to its destination; it does not prove a recipient accepted the task or that the underlying model result was independently verified.
- **UNASSIGNED:** an offer/review exists, but an implementer has not receipted acceptance. Silence and proximity are not assignments.
- **HIT / MISS / VOID:** historical labels for scored predictions; a MISS is **retained negative evidence**. Mark's Stage-34 interpretation treats the misses as material for falsification and refinement, **without regrading, erasing, or automatically calling the revised explanation validated**. [Owner decision](https://github.com/MarkDude-design/precipient/issues/1#issuecomment-6468920501).
- **PRE-EXPERIMENTAL:** descriptive, instrument-building research by a small, involved team, without a controlled independent scientific validation claim.
