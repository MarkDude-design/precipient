Name + Concept
The Mended Seam — a hat that wears its repair openly, the way a good maker does.

The Story
Nothing you make is ever finished the first time. The mended seam is the line where something broke and someone chose to fix it rather than throw it away. This hat honors the person who picks up the needle after the tear — not to hide the damage, but to make the repair part of the thing. Makers know: the mend is often stronger than the original cloth.

Physical Spec
Blank: Classic dad hat (six-panel, curved brim, adjustable strap-back)
Base color: Natural unbleached cotton (off-white / ecru) — the color of cloth before it's dyed
Front placement: A single horizontal stitch line across the center of the front panel, approximately 3 inches wide, positioned 1 inch above the brim
Embroidery description: One running stitch line in warm rust thread (Pantone 7579 C approx), with three small perpendicular cross-stitches at irregular intervals along the line — like a visible hand-mend. The crosses are ¼ inch tall, spaced unevenly (not decorative, just honest). No text. No logo.
Thread colors: Rust (1 color only)
Stitch type: Satin stitch for the main line; small cross-stitch accents. Estimated 200–300 stitch count — cheap to run.
Back: Blank. No branding. The hat speaks for itself.

Name + Concept
First Light — a hat for the maker who shows up before the world wakes.

The Story
There's a hour before the phone starts ringing, before the emails pile up, before anyone else is awake. That's when the real work happens — the quiet hour at the bench, the first cut, the first line of code, the first stitch. This hat is for that hour. It doesn't brag about early rising; it just marks the time when making is still possible, before the day decides what you'll do.

Physical Spec

Base color: Deep navy (midnight blue, not black — there's still light in it)
Front placement: A simple arc above a thin horizontal line, centered on the front panel, positioned 1 inch above the brim. The arc represents sunrise; the line represents the horizon. Total width: 2.5 inches. Total height: 1 inch.
Embroidery description: The horizon line is a single straight stitch in pale gold thread (warm, not metallic). The sunrise arc is a smooth curve in the same pale gold, rising from the horizon line at both ends. No rays, no sun disk, no text. Just the shape of light coming over the edge of the world.
Thread colors: Pale gold (1 color only)
Stitch type: Straight stitch for the horizon; satin stitch for the arc. Estimated 150–250 stitch count — very cheap to run.

Hat Concept 3: The Quiet Bench

Name + Concept
The Quiet Bench — a hat for the workshop where things get made slowly.

The Story
Every maker has a bench — the place where the work happens, where the tools live, where the half-finished things wait for you to come back. It's not glamorous. It's not a stage. It's just the place where you sit down and do the work, day after day, until the thing is done. This hat honors the bench, not the breakthrough. The work is in the sitting down.

Physical Spec
Blank: Cuffed beanie (knit, folded cuff) — warmer than a dad hat, fits the "bench" vibe
Base color: Charcoal grey (heathered, not solid — the color of a workshop that's been used)
Front placement (on the cuff): A simple rectangular bench shape, centered, 2 inches wide × 0.75 inches tall. The bench is just a flat top (the work surface) with two short legs. No tools on it, no person sitting on it. Just the bench.
Embroidery description: The bench top is a horizontal rectangle in warm brown thread (wood color). The two legs are short vertical lines in the same brown. That's it. No text, no logo, no decoration. The bench is the hat.
Thread colors: Warm brown (1 color only)
Stitch type: Satin stitch for the bench top; straight stitch for the legs. Estimated 100–200 stitch count — extremely cheap to run.
Back: Blank.
